What this is

BidTown is an isometric ad-tech town that plays one scripted day of 24 ad opportunities and computes a real campaign's response to it. A bid request leaves The Page, passes the Gatehouse (audience, frequency, budget), gets a price at the Bidding Office from pCTR x pCVR x target CPA, races two scripted rivals in the Auction Hall under second price, and only if it wins is the creative served, billed, clicked, maybe converted, and folded back into the estimates at the Performance Office.

One opportunity here is a block of 1,000 impressions from one placement, so a small budget and a $30 CPA target fit inside a 24-lap day.

How much of it is real

Genuinely computed, live, in your browser: the three gate checks; the per-user frequency counts; the smoothed estimates with their priors; the target-CPA bid and its clamp; the auction outcome and the runner-up clearing price, rounded to cents; the billing; the counters; every metric identity (CPM, CTR, CVR, CPC, CPA, ROAS); the pacing arithmetic; and the update that moves the next bid. All of it lives in js/model.js, checked against hand-derived values in test/model.test.mjs.

Scaled: one opportunity is a 1,000-impression block rather than a single impression, so a 24-lap day can carry real dollars; the auction logic itself is unchanged.

Assumed: the $45 average order value; the prior pseudo-counts; the bid clamp; hard-cap pacing instead of smooth probabilistic pacing; second price instead of first price with bid shading; no floor price; last-click attribution only.

Scripted, not simulated: the rival bids, the audience flags, the user map and the clicks and conversions the audience produces. The script is fixed data in js/model.js; the campaign's response to it is arithmetic. The audience is not a real audience and the creative is not a real creative.

Deliberately faked: the buildings, the roads and the fountain. Nothing numeric: every number on the panel and on the map comes out of the machine.

Pacing

The first time the van reaches a station it stops for long enough to read that station's write-up, between 9 and 26 seconds, with a progress bar under the panel text. Once every district has been explained the remaining laps run at a watchable pace. Space holds any stop indefinitely, S steps one station at a time, and the Speed slider scales everything, reading stops included. Reset (⟲) replays the slow tour; Run keeps what you have already read.

Controls and levers

  • Space play / pause · S next station · R reset and replay the tour · F follow camera · L labels
  • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click any station for its write-up.
  • Daily budget: the pacing cap. At the defaults the day stops at 11 blocks and two conversions; raise it and the twelfth block carries a conversion the default could not afford.
  • Target CPA: scales the bid through the formula. On a fixed budget, bidding higher wins more of the same auctions and spends the cap on fewer, dearer blocks.
  • Freq cap: the per-user block limit. Lower it and the gate turns more sessions away.
  • Changing a lever restarts the day on the new settings; what you have already read stays read.
